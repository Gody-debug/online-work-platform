Data Entry Assistant

Enter and organise information in spreadsheets and online forms.

KSh 850 Apply

Requirements: Basic spreadsheet skills, accurate typing, attention to detail, and the ability to follow written instructions.

Deliverables: A completed spreadsheet or online form using the supplied data. Keep the original column headings and formatting unless instructed otherwise.

Quality check: Check for missing entries, duplicate records, spelling errors, and incorrect values before submission.

Estimated time: 1–3 hours, depending on the number of records.

Audio Transcription

Convert supplied audio recordings into accurate written transcripts.

KSh 1,250 Apply

Requirements: Good listening skills, accurate typing, and proficiency in the language used in the recording.

Deliverables: A written transcript in the requested format. Include speaker labels or timestamps only when requested.

Quality check: Replay unclear sections and check names, punctuation, and spelling. Mark genuinely inaudible sections instead of guessing.

Estimated time: 2–4 hours per 30 minutes of audio, depending on audio clarity.
